# Five-minute city demonstration

1. Open the City explorer. Point out 36 synthetic land parcels, 1,044 active spatial units in 2026, two wards and four land-use categories.
2. Select Split. The same persistent parcel appears in the cadastral footprint and the 3D city. Select a different parcel on the 2D map, then use Focus to move the 3D camera to it.
3. Open Aranya Heights / P-9471. Select Floor 8 and a unit. Read its 3D identifier, parent land identity, area and volume. The selected structure is cut away above the chosen floor.
4. Select 2028: the structure is damaged. Select 2029: the building disappears while the parcel remains. Select 2031: a replacement commercial structure appears. Historical versions can still be reopened.
5. Validate the 2031 parcel. Geometry checks use volume coordinates; the rights check reports three unresolved replacement unit records, and source provenance remains under review because this is synthetic data.
6. Open the Parcel registry and search by parcel, building or spatial ID. Inspect a result to return to the same site in the explorer.
7. For an administrator-led demo, sign in with the configured officer credential, raise a review and resolve it in Review queue. Open Audit trail to inspect the payload and linked hashes. Reload to demonstrate database persistence.
8. Export city. The CityJSON download includes parcel surfaces and unit solids for the selected year using local engineering coordinates in metres.

Do not represent demo identifiers as official ULPIN issuance, source labels as signed documents, or review resolution as legal adjudication. AI extraction, imagery ingest and authoritative legal workflows are future integrations.
